Split Pages · Per-variant (for local clone)

A · Floating Arc Dock

Curved bottom-center bar, 56 px touch targets, active item lifts & glows. Best for the JARVIS aesthetic; weakest for power users who like persistent labels.

B · Radial Summon

Long-press / two-finger-tap anywhere brings up a radial wheel at the touch point. Most futuristic, most gesture-native; needs a discoverability hint.

C · Edge-Swipe Rail

Sidebar collapses to a luminous edge. Hover or swipe in to reveal a fat rounded rail with icons + labels. Closest to current mental model.

D · Orbital HUD

Nav items orbit the central particle sphere as glowing nodes. Most cinematic; on sub-pages they'd collapse into a small floating cluster (not shown).
